Human-in-the-Loop AI Workflows

How I use custom prompt framing to accelerate drafting without losing editorial oversight.

To handle high-volume product catalogs (such as 1,100-word e-commerce guides), I designed structured prompt frameworks that reduced initial drafting time from ~45 minutes down to ~13 minutes per guide (a ~70% time reduction). AI is used strictly for structural outlines and first drafts; human editing guarantees factual accuracy and natural phrasing.

Step 1: Structural Prompting

Configured system prompts with explicit parameters (specifications, mandatory sections, target audience) to output structured initial drafts.

Step 2: Human Fact-Checking

Manually verified all technical specifications, supply chain terms, and legal claims against source materials.

Step 3: Line Editing

Polished phrasing, removed repetitive language, and formatted copy according to internal brand style guides.
